# Taste guide

Taste is the right amount of life, with high craft. Use this guide on each direction and on each section.

## The scale

Each page sits on a scale from dead to loud. The target is the middle: polished, alive, and calm.

| Position | Signs | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Dead | A visible grid, equal boxes, no focal point, nothing moves. The user says "boring" or "meh". | Add one bold composition idea that tells the story of the product. |
| Too subtle | A soft light or a blur that nobody notices. The user says "you did almost nothing". | Add one living system that a visitor notices at the first look. |
| Target | One focal point. One living system. Every element has a job. | Keep it. Raise the craft of type, space, and motion. |
| Loud | Many moving parts, labels, counters, and effects that compete. The user says "too aggressive". | Remove elements until one focal point stays. |

## The focal point

- The hero MUST have exactly one focal point, such as the product and the headline.
- Squint at the screenshot. When two areas fight for the eye, remove one or calm one.
- No element MAY cut the focal object. Example: a background band with a straight edge that cuts a phone in two.

## The living system

A living system is one set of motion that tells the product story. Example: view icons drift toward the logo and turn into green checks, because the product pays only for verified views.

- Use one living system for the hero. Do not add a second one.
- Draw it from the product: its icons, its media, its logo.
- Keep a quiet zone around the headline, the lede, and the calls to action. Nothing moves through it.
- Make the motion slow and varied. No two items move in sync. No loop is visible. No items clump.
- Judge the motion on a recorded video, not on a still frame.

## The anti-list

These elements made rejected directions look ordinary or loud. Do not use them unless the user asks:

- chips, badges, tickers, marquees, and tapes
- dot grids, tick marks, network lines, and dashed paths
- extra counters and fake live activity
- exposed grid lines, mainly when they cross the horizontal rules of a section
- boxes with no job, and icons from an emoji set

## Craft

- Use one line system per page. Vertical grid lines and horizontal section rules fight when they meet.
- Make a highlight look typeset, not pasted. Fit it to the x-height and the baseline.
- Do not show a large "0" at the start. It reads as "zero results".
- Close dead bands. The copy column reads as one block, optically centered against the hero media.
- Anchor each small control, such as a pause button, to the grid of the page.
- Keep the lede at 55 to 62 characters per line.
- Make the navigation bar part of the design, with the brand assets. A plain bar looks unfinished.

## Motion and access

- Animate only `transform` and `opacity`. Record each exception in the spec.
- Give the page a pause control for motion that runs longer than 5 seconds.
- With reduced motion, and without JS, show a static composition that still looks designed.
- Keep the text contrast at WCAG AA or higher over every background.

## Taste check

Answer each question on the 1440 × 900 screenshot, the 375 × 812 screenshot, and the video. Fix each "no".

1. Is there exactly one focal point?
2. Does each element lose meaning when you remove it?
3. Does the page show real proof in the first screen?
4. Does the hero media tell the same story as the rest of the page?
5. Does the page look alive at the first look, without noise?
6. Does nothing cut the focal object, and does nothing cross the copy?
7. Does the page avoid every item of the anti-list?
8. Would a design director stop to look at it?
