# Spec template

Write the design spec with these sections, in this order. Write in the language that the user chose for documents. Keep every section. When a section has no content, write "none" and the reason.

```markdown
# <Page name>: design

## Goal
Who visits, the single action, and what the visitor understands in 5 seconds.

## Proof
The real content that proves the main claim, and where each item comes from.

## Direction
The name of the chosen direction, its pitch, and the parts mixed in from other directions.
The path of each prototype and screenshot.

## Brand
The logo, the color tokens, the fonts, and the assets. Give each color as a token.

## Sections
One subsection for each section of the page, in order:
- its job, in one sentence
- its content, with the exact copy
- its layout at desktop and at mobile
- its motion, and its static version

## Living system
The one living system of the hero: what moves, why, how fast, and the quiet zone.

## Honesty
Each claim with its source. Each example number with its label.
The pitch mode rules, when the page is a pitch.

## Taste checks
The 8 questions of the taste check, plus each check that this page needs.
The screenshot sizes and the video that prove each check.

## Constraints
The stack, the host, the languages, the content format, and each exception to the motion rules.

## Open items
Each missing asset or decision, with its owner.
```

The build checks its work against the taste checks of the spec. Each check needs a screenshot or a video as proof.
